HOW TO PLAY

    Flyin Roadster — Supersonic Flying Car Simulator

    Inspired by the new Tesla Roadster reveal and its SpaceX-style cold-gas thrusters, Fly, Roadster! lets you drive a flying Roadster in your browser. Launch off a giant vertical ramp, spin through the air and land on a neon city road. The car accelerates by itself: change lanes, fire the air thrusters to fly over traffic jams, bounce across car roofs and send traffic flying.

    Eat at least 5 giant Elon Musk heads — each one gives you a turbo boost — then hit the Mars launch pad at 3 km to blast off to Mars and land in the Musk Empire. Miss them and you fall back to Earth.
